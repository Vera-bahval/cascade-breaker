## Pre-freeze issue: N14

The frozen protocol literally directs unknown whenever Payments availability is unresolved, even when qualifying verification is actually observed. This can yield inconsistent requirement-level outcomes for a fulfilled conditional obligation. See `N14_CLARIFICATION.md` and `labels/n14-evidence-review.jsonl` for a proposed clarification and 14 case-specific recommendations, including the failed call and potentially unsupported classifications. Explicitly record the clarification/version and adjudicator disposition before final freeze; preserve original decisions and do not silently modify the frozen protocol.
